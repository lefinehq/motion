# Labels and relative times

The fifth step of the motion vocabulary, second half. Every time in a scene is absolute today: a cut-down that moves the call to action moves every layer around it by hand, and a sequence of layers repeats each one's end as the next one's start.

## Design

**Labels.** A composition, the scene's root included, may name moments of its timeline:

```json
"labels":{"intro":"0.5s","cta":"6s","outro":"cta+2s"}
```

- A label name is a lowercase letter followed by lowercase letters, digits and `_`. It has no `-`, so `cta-6f` always reads as `cta` minus six frames.
- A label is a time, or another label of the same composition moved by a time. Labels may refer to each other in any order, but not in a circle.

**Relative times.** A layer's `offset` and the ends of its `span` may be written against a base:

| Written | Means |
|---|---|
| `2s`, `-6f` | an absolute time, as before |
| `cta`, `cta+12f`, `cta-0.5s` | a label of the layer's composition, moved |
| `<`, `<+6f` | where the layer before it in the list ends, moved |
| `<<`, `<<-3f` | where the layer before it starts, moved |

```json
{"id":"title","text":"Hello","span":"intro-cta"},
{"id":"card","comp":"card","offset":"<-6f"},
{"id":"cta","text":"Try it","span":"cta-outro+1s"}
```

- **The layer before** is the previous layer in the same list. It starts at its span's start, or at its `offset` without a span. It ends at its span's end; without a span, a `comp` layer ends when its composition does (its length run at its stretch), and any other layer at the end of the timeline. The first layer has no layer before it, and `<` there is an error.
- **A span** is `<start>-<end>` as before. A `-` right after a base, followed by a number, moves the base; otherwise it separates the ends. `intro-cta` runs from `intro` to `cta`; `intro-6f-cta` from six frames before `intro` to `cta`. If reading the `-` as a move leaves the span without an end (`intro-2s`), it separates the ends.
- **Units.** A base and its move add in the unit they share, and in frames of the composition when they differ.
- **When.** Times resolve when the scene is read, so everything after (`at`, `check`, rendering) sees plain times. The file keeps what was written, so an edit to a label moves every layer written against it, and an edit to one layer moves the layers after it that follow it with `<`.
- **Errors** name the unknown label with the composition's labels, a `<` on the first layer, a label in a circle, a label name that breaks the rule, and a span that ends before it starts once resolved.
- **The scene model** keeps each composition's resolved labels (`Comp::labels`), for tools.

**Not in this step.**
- Labels in `in`'s `@` and in `anim` ranges: those times are the layer's own, which a label on the composition's timeline would have to be mapped into through the layer's offset and stretch. They come when a scene needs them.
- The import keeps writing absolute times.

## Tests

- Each base and move resolves, in both units and mixed.
- Labels that refer to labels resolve in any order; a circle is an error that names it.
- `<` and `<<` follow the previous layer's span, its offset, a composition's length at its stretch, or the timeline's end.
- Spans read their `-` as described, including the fallback.
- A plain scene reads exactly as before.
- The compat corpus uses labels, every base and a relative span.

## As built

Built as designed, with these notes:

- **`motion_format::when`** holds `Base`, `Moment` and `Moments` with their grammar and printing. A span tries the plain grammar first, so a plain span reads and fails exactly as before; only a span with a base reads the relative grammar.
- **Layers are read in order,** each with the extent of the one before it, so `<` and `<<` resolve as the list is read. A composition's length comes from its canvas, now read with its labels before any layer.
- **Units.** A move of nothing keeps its base's unit, so `cta` stays `6s` rather than becoming 180 frames.
- **Errors** name an unknown label with the composition's labels (or say it has none), a label circle as `a → b → a`, a label that names a layer base, a bad label name, and `<` on a first layer.
- **Compat corpus:** `labels.json` uses scene and composition labels, labels written against later labels, every base, a fallback span and a composition at a stretch.
