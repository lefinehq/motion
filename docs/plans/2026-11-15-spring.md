# Spring curves

The second step of the motion vocabulary, after [ease families](2026-11-15-ease-families.md).

## Design

- **Form:** `spring(bounce)` or `spring(bounce,perceived)`, where bounce lies in (-1, 1) and perceived is a time (`10f`, `.5s`, default 0.5 s).
- **Physics:** a damped oscillator released from rest at 0, settling at rest on 1. The perceived duration is one period of the undamped spring, so ω0 = 2π / perceived.
  - The damping ratio is ζ = 1 − bounce for a bounce of 0 or more, else 1 / (1 + bounce). The shape of the motion depends on ζ alone.
  - The residual, 1 − x(t), with ω0 = 1:
    - underdamped: e^(−ζt)(cos ωd t + (ζ/ωd) sin ωd t), with ωd = √(1 − ζ²);
    - critical: (1 + t)e^(−t);
    - overdamped: the sum of its two exponentials.
- **Settle time:** the first time after which the residual stays under 1/2000 of the change.
  - **Underdamped:** the residual's extremes fall at kπ/ωd with heights e^(−ζkπ/ωd). So the last crossing lies between the last peak at or above the threshold and the next zero, where the residual is monotonic, and it is found by bisection there.
  - **Critical and overdamped:** the residual only falls, so the crossing is found by bisection after doubling to bracket it.
  - **Why not stepping:** stepping in 20 ms increments until the motion has rested for 200 ms is approximate. It also stretches every spring by 200 ms of stillness.
- **In a span:** the spring's settle time is scaled to the span. Progress is x(p·T) + p·(1 − x(T)), so it starts at 0 and ends exactly at 1, and the leftover under the threshold is spread evenly.
- **In a behaviour without a duration:** the spring lasts its settle time, in the perceived duration's unit. A duration that is given wins.
- **Where it lives:** the math is in `motion-format::spring`, because a behaviour's duration needs it while it is parsed. `motion-scene` evaluates `Ease::Spring` with the same code.

## Not in this step

- A starting velocity, which would let a spring carry on from the speed arriving at the key.
- Mass, stiffness and damping as inputs. Bounce and perceived duration cover the same space.

## As built

- **Tests:**
  - every bounce from −0.9 to 0.95 starts at 0 and ends at 1;
  - the settle time is the last time the residual leaves the threshold: something is still moving just before it, and nothing again for 20 settle-times after;
  - an underdamped spring's peak matches 1 + e^(−ζπ/√(1−ζ²));
  - critical and overdamped springs never overshoot.
- **`compat/v1/springs.json`, checked with `motion at`:**
  - `spring(.6)` over 1.5 s from 60 to 560 peaks at 685.4 on the frames (the theory gives 687 between frames) and rests by frame 36;
  - `spring(0)` reaches 560 without overshooting.
