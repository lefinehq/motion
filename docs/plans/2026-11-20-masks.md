# Masks

**Goal:** a layer's masks cut what it draws ([D55](../design/decisions.md#d55-masks-are-items-that-cut-a-layers-content-before-its-effects)), so the import can carry the masks of a project instead of reporting them:

```json
{"id":"photo", "image":"photo.jpg", "box":"1920x1080",
 "mask":[{"mask":"M100,100 L900,100 L900,700 L100,700 Z", "feather":40},
         {"mask":"M400,300 C500,300 500,500 400,500 Z", "mode":"subtract"}]}
```

## What the corpus says

Measured on the 206 local sample projects (counts only):

- **How common.** 1,313 masks on 280 layers in 58 projects. A layer has one mask in 255 cases; 15 layers have four or more.
- **Modes.**
  - `difference` is the most common, with 1,020 masks, nearly all on the 15 layers that hold many masks.
  - Then `add` (207), `subtract` (68) and `none` (18).
  - 24 masks are inverted.
- **Animation.** 478 paths are keyed and 830 are still. 450 opacities are keyed, nearly all on the many-mask layers.
- **Feather and expansion.** 38 masks feather and 23 expand; the rest hold their defaults.
- **Effect on fidelity.** In the corpus report, masks block 43 samples (217 occurrences). This puts them in the top ten.

## The format

- **The key.** `mask` is a list of items of the one kind `mask`.
  - **The path.** Its primary key is the path, SVG path data in the layer's own px, as a shape's `path` is written.
  - **Animatable values:**
    - `path`, whose keys keep their vertex count and closure, as a shape path's do;
    - `feather`, in px, one number or `[x, y]`;
    - `opacity`, 0 to 1;
    - `expand`, in px, negative to shrink.
  - **Settings:**
    - `mode`: `add`, `subtract`, `intersect`, `lighten`, `darken`, `difference` or `none`;
    - `keep`: `inside` or `outside`, what the mask covers.
- **Combining.** The masks combine in order into one coverage, as D55 says.
  - With `a` the coverage so far and `m` a mask's own coverage times its opacity, turned to `1 − m` when it keeps the outside:
    - `add` gives `a + m − a·m`;
    - `subtract` gives `a·(1 − m)`;
    - `intersect` gives `a·m`;
    - `lighten` and `darken` give the larger and smaller of the two;
    - `difference` gives `|a − m|`.
  - The coverage starts at 0 when the first mask that is not `none` adds, lightens or differs, and at 1 otherwise.
- **Order of drawing.** The layer's content is drawn, cut by the coverage, then its effects run, then its matte and opacity apply.
- **Addresses.** Edits and checks reach a mask as they reach an effect: `photo.mask[0].feather=20`. `check` warns about an open path, which a mask closes.

## Drawing

- **Coverage.** Each mask is filled, antialiased, on a canvas of the layer's frame, through the layer's transform. The coverages then combine as above.
- **Feather.** The mask's own coverage is blurred by a gaussian whose standard deviation is half the feather, along each axis. The factor is to be measured against the corpus, as the particles' units were.
- **Expansion.** The mask's coverage grows or shrinks by `expand` px, a distance from its edge, before it feathers.
- **Order.** The cut coverage multiplies the layer's content before its effects run (D49 gives effects that read pixels a canvas of their own).

## The import

- **Decoding.** The decoder reads each mask triplet of the `ADBE Mask Parade` group: the `ADBE Mask Atom`, its `mkif` (inverted, motion blur, falloff and mode, at 0x00 to 0x04), and its group.
- **Paths.**
  - A path is in the mask space. That is fractions of the source size on a layer with a source, and px on a text or shape layer. The import turns it into the layer's px, and keyed paths into keys.
  - A mask with no stored path is the whole mask space, as a rectangle.
- **Default values.** A property the project leaves out holds its default:
  - feather 0;
  - opacity 100%;
  - expansion 0.
- **What is reported.** The report lists:
  - masks it cannot hold yet: motion blur, linear falloff, RotoBezier paths whose tangents it does not compute;
  - masks on a layer that does not draw.

## Steps

Each step is one pull request, with its tests.

1. **Decision.** D55 and this plan.
2. **The format.** The `mask` key, its item kind and settings, parsing, validation, tracks, edits by address, and the evaluated masks of a frame.
3. **Drawing.** Coverage, the modes and their starting coverage, inversion, opacity, feather and expansion, before effects. Golden tests for each mode and for feather.
4. **The import.** `mkif`, paths in mask space, keyed paths and the defaults. Then measured on the corpus, with the feather factor fitted there.
5. **Docs.** `scene-format.md`, `aep-import.md`, `features.md` and the CHANGELOG.

## Out of scope

- Mask motion blur and linear feather falloff.
- Variable-width feather, the per-vertex feather points of newer projects.
- Masks on shape groups. A shape layer's group can mask with a merge instead.
