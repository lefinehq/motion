# Generated variants: `nakkash vary`

**Goal:** any master, imported or written, yields variants along palette, type, content, timing and format, and can be improved by the check's own repairs ([D78](../design/decisions.md#d78-generated-variants-move-along-measured-axes-and-pass-when-they-add-no-error)). Each variant is checked against its master and rendered:

```text
$ nakkash vary launch.json --seed 7 --count 4 --axis palette --axis format=9:16 --out vary
v1  palette rotate 112°            check OK  KNOWN 3  vary/v1/film.mp4
v2  palette complementary 31°      check OK  KNOWN 3  vary/v2/film.mp4
v3  format 1080x1920, 3 groups     check OK  KNOWN 2  vary/v3/film.mp4
v4  format 1080x1920, 2 groups     new: E offscreen hero/title 40-52f +18px
3 passed, 1 failed  ·  vary/sheet.png
```

## Command

`nakkash vary <scene> [--spec vary.json] [--seed N] [--count N] [--axis <axis>[=<value>]]... [--improve] [--dry-run] [--json] --out <dir>`

- `--axis` may repeat; each variant takes one value of each axis given, drawn from the seed. `--count` variants are made, numbered `v1` to `vN`.
- A spec file says the same with parameters: brand colours, schemes, font families, copy or a data file, a duration, a tempo or a music track, a pace, sizes. Flags given with a spec override it.
- `--improve` runs the repairs after the axes; on its own it improves the master.
- `--dry-run` writes the scenes and their reports and renders nothing.

```json
{"seed":7,"count":6,
 "palette":{"schemes":["rotate","analogous","complementary"],"brand":["#0F62FE"]},
 "type":{"pairs":"contrast","copy":"long"},
 "content":{"rows":"rows.csv"},
 "timing":{"duration":"15s","music":"track.wav","pace":"snappy"},
 "format":{"sizes":["9:16","1:1","4:5"]},
 "improve":true}
```

## Output

```text
vary/
  report.json            every variant: recipe, outcome, new findings, files
  sheet.png              the master and each variant, a row of frames each
  master.check.txt       the master's report, the baseline
  v1/scene.json          the variant, readable by every command
  v1/recipe.json         the axes and values it was made from, and the seed
  v1/check.txt           its full report, then what is new over the master
  v1/film.mp4            its render (not with --dry-run)
  v1/sheet.png           its frames
```

## How each axis works

**Palette.** The scene's colours are gathered with the place of each use: slot values, layer, item and effect colours, keys of colour tracks, text runs. Colours within a small distance are one palette entry, weighted by uses. A scheme gives each chromatic entry a new hue in OKLCH; each entry keeps its WCAG relative luminance, and its chroma is cut back until it is in gamut. Neutral entries stay neutral. A brand colour takes the entry of most weight it is closest to in hue and lightness; every text colour read against it is then moved in lightness until the pair's ratio is the master's, or 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24 px), whichever is lower. Colour slots change once; values that follow them follow, including those D77 derives from an entry (`"$accent l-0.021"`), which keep their difference.

**Type.** A face is classified from its OS/2 and post tables: the family class and PANOSE serif style, fixed pitch, weight, width class, and the average width and x-height measured from its glyphs. A pairing rule (`same`, `contrast`) picks, by the seed, a family of the matching class for the headline role or the largest text, and one for the rest. Fitting measures the new text with the new face at the master's size and compares it with the master's box at rest: above the box, it is set smaller, down to 60 % of its size; past that it is wrapped into the fewest balanced lines that fit, then set smaller again; a read longer than the text's hold is held longer by the time map.

**Content.** A row is a variant. A column names `$slot`, `@role` or `address.key`. An image is fitted inside the box of the one it replaces, keeping its aspect.

**Timing.** The master is evaluated frame by frame, and each frame's activity is how far its layers' boxes, opacities and colours move from the frame before. The time map gives frames with activity a rate of 1 and spreads the change of length over the rest, in proportion to how still they are, with a rate between 1/3 and 3. A beat grid comes from a tempo and offset, or from the onsets of a music track (a spectral-flux envelope and its autocorrelation); the moments where moves start, and labels, are moved to the nearest beats in order. A pace scales the length of every move (0.75 snappy, 1.33 calm) and gives the difference to the holds, so the length stays. The map is a `remap` track with a key at every change of rate. Sound inside the master is muted and a music track, if given, plays at the root.

**Format.** The master is evaluated and walked as `check` walks it. A layer is background when its box covers most of the canvas for most of the time it shows. The others are content; a `comp` layer covering the frame is opened, so its layers count, not it. Content layers whose boxes over time overlap or nearly touch are one group. Each group is scaled to fit the new safe area, no larger than the short side's ratio, and placed at its relative position there; groups that then overlap while both show are stacked in their order across and down, and the stack is scaled to fit. Each group is one instance of the master with its transform; one more instance shows the backgrounds, scaled to cover, centred on the busiest window of the drawn backgrounds (an edge-energy map of a few frames).

**Improve.** The repairs, each keyed to a rule:

| Rule | Repair |
|---|---|
| `judder` | `mblur` on the layer |
| `easing` | the constant-speed keys of the layer's move get `in-out-cubic`, or `out-cubic` when the move enters |
| `contrast` | the text's fill moves in lightness, hue kept, until the ratio passes |
| `text_hold` | the time map holds the span longer |

A repair is kept when the report after it has nothing new over the report before it, rules and gates both, and the targeted finding is gone or measured better.

## Gating

A variant passes when its report, rules and gates, has no error its master's lacks ([D78](../design/decisions.md#d78-generated-variants-move-along-measured-axes-and-pass-when-they-add-no-error)). `Report::since` takes an address map, so a finding of `title` in the master matches one of `group-2/title` in the variant; a measure in px is compared after the group's scale. `batch` uses the same gate.

## Steps

Each step is one PR on `development`, with tests and docs.

1. D78 and this plan.
2. Gating: one baseline gate for `batch` and `vary` (rules and gates of the master, once); the address map in `Report::since`.
3. `vary` itself: recipe, seeds, output, sheets, and the palette axis.
4. Type and content.
5. Timing: the time map, beat grids and pace.
6. Format: groups, placement and backgrounds.
7. Improve.
8. Fixes found by the corpus run, and this plan's status.

## Measuring

Every scored sample of the corpus gets four variants: palette, type with long copy, 9:16 (16:9 for a master that is already portrait), and a retime to two thirds of its length. The table is the pass rate per axis before and after the fixes, and each failure is a bug fixed or a limit measured and explained.
