## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Scaled characters: the character keys `horizontal-scale` and `vertical-scale`, above 0, 1 by default. A horizontal scale sets the characters wider or narrower: each advance, the tracking after it and the glyph scale across by it. A vertical scale draws the glyphs taller or shorter, up from their baseline. The spacing from one baseline to the next stays. The import maps a character style's scales, which it reported before ([evidence S1–S2](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#scaled-characters)).
  - Lines within 0.25 px of their stored layout go from 26 to 56 of 129 over 13 samples. m640 goes from 17.20 % to 10.08 % of its content failing over all frames (content error 0.0892 → 0.0140), m641 from 44.96 % to 29.36 % (0.2737 → 0.1186), m634 from 44.65 % to 40.80 % with one more frame passing over its floor, and m620 from 33.66 % to 32.97 %. m639 goes from 56.31 % to 64.50 %: the reference skews its titles, which the scene does not draw yet.
- Text options of the whole text: three `style` keys of a new text level, which only the whole text has. `anchor-by` gives every character the anchor of its word, its line or the whole text instead of its own (`char`, the default), so a word scales and turns as one; `anchor-align` moves every anchor from the middle of its baseline, across by fractions of its part's width and down by fractions of its font's size; `paint-across` stacks fills and strokes across characters, each in its own `paint` order (`each`, the default), every fill over every stroke (`fills-over`) or every stroke over every fill (`strokes-over`). The import maps `ADBE Text More Options` to them, and blending between characters only where it blends nothing ([evidence M1–M4](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#text-options-of-the-whole-text)); a constant expression on an option is taken as its value.
  - 85 reports in 23 scored samples are gone. The drawing changes only in m815 (99.85 % → 99.84 % of its content failing over all frames) and m816 (62.96 % → 62.95 %); the other text samples measured draw the same to the fourth decimal.
- `--threads N` draws frames on at most N threads, for every command that renders. Each thread holds the frame it draws, and a frame with 3D planes seen up close holds about a gigabyte, so the peak follows the threads: 90 such frames of a 9:16 film peaked at 7.2 GB on 4 threads and 4.3 GB on 2.
- A fidelity sample may give the background its reference shows (`bg`, a colour as a scene's `bg` takes it, such as `"#000000"`), and is measured over it in place of the project's. Some references show the composition's background colour where nothing covers it, others pure black, and no project data tells the two apart, so the override belongs to the measurement: the import and the engine do not change. The ledger records the colour per sample (`Measure::bg`, absent when the project's was used, so earlier ledgers read as before), `motion fidelity` prints it under the sample and lists such samples after the score, and comparing with a ledger measured over another background names the sample. A background that is not a colour is refused. On the local corpus, the three samples whose previews show black over their composition colour (m558, m592, m811), measured over `#000000`: mean content error over all frames 0.0515 → 0.0382, 0.1173 → 0.0432 and 0.0560 → 0.0136; failing 19.51% → 8.47%, 42.06% → 34.67% and 75.12% → 22.41%; frames passing over the codec floor 0 → 2 of 11, 2 → 3 of 20 and 0 → 0 of 14. m800's preview shows its colour, and over black it would err 0.0673 → 0.2588.
- `motion-postscript`, a PostScript language interpreter for EPS footage ([D71](docs/design/decisions.md#d71-eps-footage-is-drawn-by-our-own-postscript-interpreter)). It runs the program in a plain or binary EPS file and returns what it paints as vector items over the whole-point bounding box, the precise box kept as where the art lies: fills in page space, strokes with their pen in their own space, pictures, each with an sRGB colour and a clip. It has the language's scanner, its four stacks, control, composite objects, files with the hexadecimal, base-85, run-length, deflate and sub-file filters, paths, clips, painting, device, indexed, separation and n-channel colour, images and image masks, and the resources and dictionaries of the second level that EPS writers' procedure sets use. Device CMYK is drawn by the language's own rule, which is what the reference renders show ([evidence E1](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#eps-footage)). Text, smooth shading, patterns, user paths and masked images are reported, not drawn; `%%EOF` ends a program, so private data after it is never read, and encrypted sections are skipped, never decoded. Runs are deterministic and bounded by an operation budget, a memory budget and depth and output limits, and end in a note, never a panic: 2 seconds for the default budget of 100 million operations in a release build. The five EPS files of the local corpus run without a note, the largest in 36 215 operations and 4 ms.
  - Vector footage reads EPS files: a row of `motion_vector::FORMATS` lays the interpreter's items on the canvas, the whole-point box as the page and the precise box as its art ([evidence E2](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#eps-footage)), so the import draws them where it reported footage in a format it cannot read. m772 fails on 19.24% → 18.48% of its content over all frames (content error 0.0149 → 0.0089), m788 on 22.38% → 22.34% (0.0130 → 0.0122), m794 on 41.20% → 41.17% (0.0197 → 0.0191), m837 on 38.16% → 37.75% (0.0392 → 0.0370), m838 on 30.04% → 29.92% (0.0176 → 0.0166); no sample passes more frames. The plain files reach what converting them to PDF outside motion and drawing that reached.
- Vector footage is drawn by motion itself ([D70](docs/design/decisions.md#d70-vector-footage-is-drawn-from-its-outlines)): an image layer's `image` may name a PDF file, or an `.ai` file that is PDF inside, recognised by its first bytes. A new crate, `motion-vector`, reads the first page into the rasteriser's display list through a PDF interpreter (`hayro-syntax`, `hayro-interpret`) and one drawing model, `Canvas`, that readers of other vector formats share (a row of `FORMATS` each): fills and strokes with either fill rule, caps, joins and dashes; device grey, RGB, CMYK and ICC-based colour converted to sRGB; transforms, clips and `q`/`Q`; constant alpha and blend modes; axial and radial shadings as gradients, others sampled; tiling patterns as vectors; Form XObjects, transparency groups and soft masks; Flate, DCT and other images; text in embedded fonts. A vector layer is drawn once at its box's size and transformed, or, with the new `continuous` key (the project's switch that collapses transformations), drawn at the output's resolution every frame. The new `crop` key shows a part of a picture, raster or vector, over the box; the import picks the crop box or the art box by the footage's size. What a file holds that cannot be drawn (fonts it does not embed, overprinting, transfer functions, knockout groups, …) is reported on each layer that shows it. The rasteriser has a gradient paint (`Paint::Gradient`). On the 10 corpus samples with `.ai` footage: m563 2 → 8 of 13 frames passing over the floor (mean error 0.0289 → 0.0208), m564 1 → 6 of 11 (0.0154 → 0.0075), m593 1 → 3 of 20, m562 0.0022 → 0.0012, m830 0.0460 → 0.0307, m803 0.0947 → 0.0923. The toolchain moves to Rust 1.92, which the interpreter needs.
- A frame passes over its codec floor only when its mean error does too ([D69](docs/design/decisions.md#d69-a-reference-frame-passes-over-its-codec-floor)): a floor file may give each reference frame its error floor (`errors`), the mean content error the encode alone causes, and a frame then passes when its failing share is at most its floor plus `DiffOptions::PASSING` and its mean content error at most its error floor plus `DiffOptions::ERROR_MARGIN` (0.004). `Floors::of` gives a `Floor` per frame, `Error::passes_over` takes one, and a floor file without `errors` holds the failing share alone, as before. The ledger records each window's mean error floor (`error_floor`), recorded, not compared; the strict rule does not change. On a photograph or small saturated colour, nearly every pixel fails on the encode alone, so the failing share passed m806 (78.2% against a floor of 80.0%) with a mean error of 0.0618 against the encode's 0.0538. The margin is the smallest that keeps passing the samples drawn right (m759, m650, m562 and m783 lie at most 0.0038 above their error floors). On the local corpus (140 scored samples, 2161 frames), under the floor rule 7 samples pass every frame (m562 m571 m649 m650 m759 m768 m783), 3 more once settled (m572 m575 m740) and 405 frames pass, against 10, 4 and 472 on the failing share alone; strictly 1, 1 and 247. Twice the margin would also pass m560 and m573 on every frame and m752 and m806 once settled.
- The `beam` effect: a straight beam `length` of the line from `start` to `end` long. Its front is `time × (1 + length)` of the way along, so with `length` 1 it spans the line at `time` 0.5. Its thickness runs from `start-thickness` to `end-thickness`, its ends are flat, and its edges fade over `softness` from `inside-color` to `outside-color`. It draws in place of the layer, or over it with `composite` on, kept to the layer's box ([evidence B1 to B3](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#beams)). The import maps `ADBE Laser` to it, where its layer drew nothing, and reports a laser without 3D perspective. The audio effects and the beam share one pen (`pen.rs`), which now also draws strokes whose thickness runs from one end to the other.

- A reference frame passes over its codec floor ([D69](docs/design/decisions.md#d69-a-reference-frame-passes-over-its-codec-floor)). A sample in the fidelity manifest may name a floor file (`floor`, read as `Floors`): the codec floor of each reference frame by its file name, the share of its content that fails the tolerance only because the reference is a lossy encode. A frame passes when its failing share is at most its floor plus `DiffOptions::PASSING` (`Error::passes_over`, `Rule::Floor`); without a floor it is 0 and the rule is the strict one. Floors that miss a reference frame or name one it lacks are refused. `motion fidelity` prints the score under the floor rule, then the strict rule (`Rule::Strict`), each with the frames passing over all scored samples (`Scoreboard::frames_passing`, `Scoreboard::frames`), and each window's line gives both counts and the mean floor. The ledger records `frames_passing_floor` and `floor` per window; a ledger written before them reads as measured with no floor, so comparing with one shows floors coming in as frames passing, and the floor itself is recorded, not compared. Comparing ledgers no longer counts a text line wholly off before and after as worse. The floors are measured by the local corpus tooling, outside the repository: our render encoded with the preview's own x264 settings (read from its stream and checked against our encode's), decoded as the references were, and compared with the scene. On the local corpus (140 scored samples, 2161 frames), under the floor rule 8 samples pass every frame (m560 m562 m571 m573 m650 m759 m768 m783), 4 more once settled (m572 m575 m740 m752) and 449 frames pass; under the strict rule 1, 1 more and 240 frames, as before. The samples' mean floors have a median of 5.92%, and 20 are above 25%, where the content is small saturated colour or photographic.

- Expanding outlines ([D68](docs/design/decisions.md#d68-an-expansion-moves-outlines-out-by-an-amount)): the `expand` shape item moves every outline listed before it in its group, those in earlier groups included, `amount` px out (to the left of the way it runs, outward for clockwise outlines; a negative amount moves in), each curve to its true parallel. Sides that part are joined by `join` `miter` (within `miter` amounts of the corner, else bevelled), `round` or `bevel`; sides that cross are cut where they cross; an open outline becomes the closed outline around it; `copies` and `copy-offset` repeat it further out. Like a trim, it changes the outline every paint draws. - Repeaters ([D67](docs/design/decisions.md#d67-a-repeater-copies-the-items-above-it-as-groups)): the `repeater` shape item draws everything listed before it in its group again, `copies` times, as if those items were one group per copy. Copy `k` stands `k + offset` steps along: moved by that many `position`s, turned by that many `rotate`s and scaled by `scale` to that power, the last two about `anchor`; the steps add up rather than compound. Its opacity runs from `start-opacity` towards `end-opacity` by `k / copies`, so the last copy stops a step short; a fractional count draws one copy more, faded by the fraction; `composite` `below` (the default) draws each copy under the one before, `above` over it. The paints before a repeater draw every copy, faded by its opacity; the trims and paints after it reach the outlines of every copy, so two repeaters in a row make a grid. `Role::Repeater` is new. The import maps `ADBE Vector Filter - Repeater` and its transform group, where it reported them as unsupported, and expressions read `copies`, `offset` and the repeater transform's `anchorPoint`, `position`, `scale`, `rotation`, `startOpacity` and `endOpacity`. Measured against the references ([evidence R1 to R3](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#repeaters)): opacity steps of `k / n`, not `k / (n − 1)`, err 0.037 against 0.080 where they differ on m647 and 0.015 against 0.033 on m769; a repeater that stores no position holds it at the origin (m564 0.315 against 0.455 for the editor's [100, 0], m565 0.337 against 0.439). - Round corners ([D66](docs/design/decisions.md#d66-round-corners-cut-sharp-corners-back-along-their-sides)): the `round-corners` shape item rounds the sharp corners of every outline listed before it in its group, those inside earlier groups included, each in its own space, for every paint that draws them. Each vertex without handles is cut back along both sides by `radius` (at most half of each side) and the cuts are joined by a cubic whose handles reach 0.5519 of the way back to the corner; smooth vertices and open ends stay. `radius` rests at 10. - Merging outlines ([D65](docs/design/decisions.md#d65-a-merge-replaces-the-outlines-above-it-by-one)): the `merge` shape item takes every outline listed before it in its group, those inside earlier groups included, through their transforms, and leaves one outline in their place; the paints listed before it no longer draw what it takes. `{"merge":"merge"}` (the default) lays the outlines one after another; `add`, `subtract`, `intersect` and `exclude` combine the areas they cover, each read by the nonzero rule, the first with each next one in turn, into simple closed contours that keep their curves. The boolean operations live in `motion-scene`'s `boolean` module, on `linesweeper` (MIT or Apache-2.0, built on `kurbo`), so later modifiers can use them. - A selector may choose each part by its own amount ([D64](docs/design/decisions.md#d64-a-selector-may-choose-each-part-by-its-own-amount)): a `parts` selector holds one `part` item per part it counts by `by`, in reading order, whose key is how strongly its part is chosen, from -1 to 1, and may be animated; a part past the last item is not chosen, and `mode` combines it with the selectors before it. - Tracks that follow a sound (audio plan step 7): `"scale":"rest -> rest*1.25 @level music 30-150Hz smooth 0.06s out-quad"` goes from its first value at silence to its second at full level. The expression bake reads them as the original's marker keys (`marker.numKeys`, `marker.key(n).time`, `.comment`, `.duration`, and `marker.key(name)` by comment; `motion_expr::Val::Marker`). Expressions that read markers, such as the duration-control rigs that time an intro and an outro by two markers, failed with `it reads a value the reader does not decode (markers)`; on the local corpus, all 42 such expressions in 9 samples now bake. `Reason::Markers` is gone. Of those 9 samples, m786 goes from 99.89% to 85.52% failing (0 → 1 of 18 frames passing) and m829 from 5.77% to 5.49%, and none draws farther; the rigs that drive time remapping wait on the scene taking it.
- The expression bake reads a 2D layer's anchor point, position and scale as two components, as the original gives them to expressions; the project keeps a third, which only a 3D layer's (and a camera's or a light's) have. A rig that walks up a layer's parents and divides its scale by theirs, `for (i = 0; i < s.length; i++) s[i] *= 100 / P.transform.scale.value[i]`, read a third component its parent's two-component scale expression did not give, made it `NaN`, and the bake kept the stored scale: the logo of a gaming lower-third family drew stretched, 7.68 by 4.32 times instead of 2 by 2. On the local corpus, of the 42 samples under 25% failing measured against the same base, 5 draw closer and none farther (all-frames failing, frames passing): m783 1.72% → 0.93%, 4 → 8 of 19; m571 7.40% → 1.85%, 2 → 5 of 17; m573 9.17% → 1.78%, 1 → 6 of 17; m572 15.33% → 12.48%, 2 → 5 of 17; m575 18.92% → 13.63%.
- `contrast` measures text inside a composition with a `bg` against that background: its glyphs were drawn with their composition, background and all, so the background across the canvas read as glyphs on themselves and any such text failed at `1:1`, ink on yellow (about 13:1) among it. The glyphs are now drawn with no composition's background, and what is behind them stays the scene's and each composition's `bg` and the layers below (`Session::list_in`).
- A 3D layer that lies wholly in front of another's plane draws over it, wherever the two centres are: the paint order sorted each run of 3D layers by the depth of each box's centre alone, so a label laid 1 px in front of a large turned card, whose centre sat deeper than the card's, drew behind it and vanished. Where the boxes of two 3D layers show over each other, the one wholly behind the other's plane now draws first, and of two in one plane the first in the list (`space::depth_order`, `View::corners`); the depth of the centres orders the rest and breaks cycles. m739's box titles ("MUSIC BOX", "ALL TRACKS NEW ARRIVALS") now draw: content failing 12.13 % → 10.93 % over all frames, 18.66 % → 15.59 % settled. Of the 54 scored corpus samples with 3D layers, the other 53 measure the same.
- A layer with a source (a composition, footage, a solid or a null) keeps its anchor point in fractions of the source's size, as the project stores it, so the import now multiplies it by that size, keys and motion-path handles too, and the expression bake reads it in px (`Place::source_unit`, `IN_SOURCE`). Read as px, a null stored at `[0.5, 0.5]` over a 100 px solid pinned its children 49.5 px down and right, and a composition stored at `[0.5, 1]`, its bottom middle, was pinned by its top-left corner. The same project exported through the host's scripting API reads `[60, 60]` for the fixture's null stored at `[0.5, 0.5]`. Of the 182 scored corpus samples, 51 import differently: 26 draw closer to their reference, 23 the same and 2 slightly further (m622 +0.24, m808 +0.34 points, a text now drawn in place in the wrong colour). Content failing, all frames, settled: m650 3.16 % → 0.96 %, 4.46 % → 1.24 %; m560 51.77 % → 6.87 %, 57.82 % → 8.50 %; m648 58.44 % → 16.55 %; m640 36.54 % → 17.20 %; m712 31.30 % → 13.64 %; m715 20.12 % → 8.55 %; m716 53.03 % → 29.72 %; m596 39.39 % → 30.46 % ([evidence](docs/design/aep-evidence.md#anchor-points)).
- Each face of a font collection (`.ttc`) sets text in itself. The typesetter registered a face's whole collection, so every face after the first was set in the collection's first face, or failed on a glyph that face lacks: none of the faces of a system collection such as Helvetica Neue, Gill Sans or Cochin past the first could set text. A face of a collection is now registered alone, as a copy of the collection whose header lists only it, made when text first uses it. On the local corpus, the five samples that name faces of macOS collections now set them: m793 (Snell Roundhand) goes from 0 to 3 of 5 texts within the stored layout and from 0.2119 to 0.1998 settled, m596 (Helvetica Neue) from a worst text 46.46 px off to 20.01, and m791's Gill Sans UltraBold sets its words at exactly the stored widths.
- An empty text layer draws as nothing instead of failing: the format keeps one base run of no character for an empty text to carry its style, and the scene handed that run to the typesetter, which takes no run of no character, so the scene could not be drawn. On the local corpus, three samples with an emptied text layer could not be measured; they now measure at 34.6%, 38.3% and 64.0% failing from when they have settled.
- An empty path reads back: the format wrote the path with no vertex as `""` and then rejected it, so a project with a path of no vertices (a shape group whose path was cleared) imported into a scene the format refused. As in SVG, `""` is now the path with no vertex, which draws nothing. On the local corpus, one sample failed to import this way; it now measures at 5.65% failing from when it has settled.
- The import reads the adjustment switch on shape and text layers, not only on solids: the layer's content says where its effects apply to what is below it, and without an effect the scene draws it draws nothing. Seven corpus samples hold 400 such shape layers, which drew as plain shapes. Mean settled error over them 0.369 → 0.329; m771 0.466 → 0.196, where a white panel hid the title.
- A null whose anchor the project leaves out rests it at its top-left corner, not its centre, so the layers it parents are no longer 50 px up and left of where the project draws them. On the local corpus, 10 samples draw closer to their reference and none passes or fails because of it; on two local `.mogrt` templates the layers now land on the preview's.
- A template's point control that drives a position kept as separate dimensions no longer writes the composition's centre over the layer's own position: the position stays as it is, and the report names the control as left out.

### Changed

- Encoding is about three times as fast for light scenes: the colour conversion reads each pixel once and converts bands of rows in parallel, 3.3 ms for a 1080p frame instead of 11.1 ms, with every sample the same. 60 frames of the launch example render to H.264 in 0.3 s.
- `--video-quality web` asks the system's H.264 and HEVC encoders for quality 0.8, up from 0.65: dark gradients no longer turn into blocks with coloured blotches (the 99th-percentile error on the demo film's night sky falls from 3.0 levels to 1.26), for about half again the size. The speed and size table in `rendering.md` is measured again, all of it one way.
- Reports name layers by address ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): `StoredText::layer` and `TextCheck::layer` are an `Address` where the scene writes the layer (`title`, or `scene.comps.card.layers[title]`), replacing `comp` and `layer`. `diff --ignore` and the fidelity manifest's `ignore` take addresses, so `card/photo` hides a layer in one instance. The import's own path `Step` is gone: sources use `motion_format::Step`, `address::json_at` and `address::key_text`, which the edit crate and `Place` share too.
- The variants design reads as motion works now ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): the project is imported directly, expressions are baked, instances of a composition are edited one at a time, and a variant can be patched back into its project. The glossary gains address, instance and override.
- Edits take a layer's address ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): `card/title text="Sale"` writes `overrides` on `card` for that instance alone, starting a change inside a key from the key as the instance draws it. A key path may pick a list element by id or index, `fx[glow].radius` or `scene comps.label.layers[title].text=Hi`. `none` through an instance takes back its own value, and refuses a key its composition gives. `Edit::target` is an `Address`, and `KeyPath` has steps instead of segments. `check` gives fix hints for inner layers as edits of their instance.
- `check` and `at` reach every layer at any depth ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): a layer inside a composition is checked in scene space and reported by its address, `card/title`, measured by what its compositions' clips let it draw. `motion at` takes addresses, and lists every layer at any depth when given none. `Violation::id` is now `Violation::address`.
- `motion import` clamps a value outside its property's range into it, as the original draws it, instead of failing: a wiggled opacity past 100%, a negative roundness, colour channels above 1. Expression samples are clamped frame by frame before the fit; the report lists each clamp under `clamped`.
- The scene's root is a composition ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): `Scene::root` and `SceneGraph::root` hold its canvas, clock, background and layers, and one function validates and compiles the root and every composition under `comps`.
- A diagnostic's path is a `Place` ([D43](docs/design/decisions.md#d43-one-layer-address-and-edits-per-instance)): an `Address` inside the scene, or text for places outside it. Errors name a layer by its id, as edits and queries do: `title.in`, not `layers[0].in`. A layer whose id is missing or shared is still named by its index.
- `motion export` compares what it reads back with the edit in the import's six-decimal steps, allowing one, so a value half a step from both neighbours no longer fails the check.
- Baking expressions sits behind `motion-import`'s `bake` feature, which only the CLI turns on: `motion-engine` renders and imports without linking the script engine. The D38 and D39 guards check each crate's resolved dependencies (`cargo tree`), not its manifest lines.
- The bake eases the values it reads with the scene's own curves and motion paths (`motion_scene::Ease`, `PathSpan`), not a copy of them.
- An expression that reads nothing that changes with time runs once, not at every frame: a run knows whether it read `time`, a value the host says changes, or randomness seeded by the time (`Run::constant`, `Host::constant`).
- Six crates instead of ten: the error type and shared grammar pieces live in `motion-format`, evaluation in `motion-scene`, and logging setup in `motion-engine`.
- `check` evaluates one frame at a time instead of keeping every frame in memory.
- Error messages name the path and the accepted form, with an example, for every syntax and unknown-name error.
- Shorthands (size, time, colour, rect, font, position, curves, `in`, `anim` tracks) are read by `winnow` grammars.
- The scene is read with a serde document, so errors in its shape carry the path of the value.
- Edit paths use a real grammar, so `card anim.opacity="0 -> 1 @0-12f"` and JSON values (`anim={...}`) are read by `serde_json`.
- Quiet by default: nothing is logged unless `--log` or `MOTION_LOG` asks for it, and a failing run prints only its `error:` line.
- `motion diff` and `motion fidelity` measure a third window, `content`: where the scene draws or the reference differs from the scene's background by more than the tolerance, so content the scene fails to draw counts against it. It is the headline number in their output, their JSON and the ledger, and the ledger's regression check counts it; `DiffOptions` takes the layers to ignore, and the diff leaves out where they draw ([D42](docs/design/decisions.md#d42-fidelity-is-headlined-over-the-reference-content)).

- `at` prints only the properties that differ from their default after `x y w h`.
- The property `x` and `y` are now the components of `position`; `scale` is a point (`scale=0.8,0.8` in `at`); `at` places the layer's anchor, which rests at the centre of its box, so existing scenes draw as before.
- `check` fix hints skip axes driven by `anim`.
- Properties live in one registry per kind of object (`LAYER`, `GROUP`, and one per shape item and effect); unknown-property errors name the kind (`unknown layer property`).
- A layer with two content keys reports the conflict at the second key.
- Text is set with `motion-text` at compile time, so boxes, `at` and `check` measure real glyphs; `compile` takes a `Typesetter` and returns a `Result`, and `ApproxTextMeasure` is gone. A text layer's own coordinates start at its first baseline, and its box is the layout's bounds. The built-in font's family is `Inter Variable`.
- Layer boxes are rectangles (`kurbo::Rect`): rests and `at` resolve in them. `Session::graph`, `frame` and `check` return a `Result`, and the session has a `fonts` step.
- Layer `fill` takes any colour form, including `[r, g, b, a]` from 0 to 1.
- `NodeState` carries what a node draws (`content`, `local_bounds`, `fill`, `fx`, `hidden`); `check` ignores hidden layers.
- Unit tests moved out of `src/` into `tests/unit`, mirroring the source tree.

### Fixed

- The import measures an effect's points on a shape or text layer from the layer's origin, which sits in the middle of the composition-sized box the project measures them in, not at its top left. Ramps, four-colour gradients, fractal noise, turbulent displacement and the transform effect on such layers now land where the original draws them: mean settled content error over 193 samples 0.2956 → 0.2944, 7 better and 3 worse by at most 0.006 (m647 0.162 → 0.042, m556 0.120 → 0.045).
