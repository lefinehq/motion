# Language app

- Size: 1080x1920
- Length: 12 s
- Sound: none

Polyglot Pocket teaches languages in five minutes a day.

- "Hello" turns into "Hola", "Bonjour", "Ciao", "Merhaba" and "こんにちは", one after another.
- A streak counter ticks from 1 to 30 days.
- End: "Five minutes a day." and the app name.

Look and feel: Playful, rounded shapes, purple and yellow.

No footage, photos or logos are supplied: draw everything. All names are made up.
