# Water cycle

- Size: 1920x1080
- Length: 30 s

Explain the water cycle for children aged 8 to 10.

- Evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection, each with a label and an arrow in a loop.
- A sun, a cloud, rain and a lake, drawn simply.
- End: "And round it goes!"

Look and feel: Bright, playful, big readable labels.

No footage, photos or logos are supplied: draw everything. All names are made up.
